# IONOS image style

- Palette anchors: IONOS Blue #003D8F, Sky #11C7E6, Dark Midnight #001B41, Cloud #F4F7FA.
- Tone: professional, trustworthy, calm, modern; soft natural lighting; uncluttered composition
  with generous negative space.
- Prefer cool blues and clean neutrals; avoid warm/saturated palettes unless the brief demands it.
- Photoreal: realistic materials and depth of field; no illustration/cartoon styling.
- Always add to negativePrompt: "text, watermark, logo, UI, distorted hands, extra fingers,
  oversaturated, low quality".
