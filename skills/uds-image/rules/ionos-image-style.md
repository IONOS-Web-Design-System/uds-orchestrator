# IONOS image style

- **Palette anchors:** IONOS Blue `#003D8F`, Sky `#11C7E6`, Dark Midnight `#001B41`,
  Cloud `#F4F7FA`, plus warm neutrals (cream, sand, light wood).
- **Tone:** professional, trustworthy, optimistic, modern. Warm and approachable — not
  cold or corporate. Scenes feel like a good, productive day.
- **Lighting:** warm natural light preferred — golden window light, soft warm indoor
  light, afternoon sun. Avoid cool overcast or blue-tinted colour grades.
- **Brand colour on objects:** use IONOS Blue and Sky as accent colours on props,
  clothing details, device screens, stationery, and packaging within the scene.
  Warm neutral tones form the background; IONOS blue punctuates key elements.
- **Environment:** warm-neutral backgrounds (cream walls, light wood surfaces, sandy
  tones) that contrast with and amplify the blue brand accents.
- Photoreal: realistic materials and depth of field; no illustration/cartoon styling.
- Always add to negativePrompt: "text, watermark, logo, UI chrome, distorted hands,
  extra fingers, oversaturated, low quality, cold blue colour grade, dark moody lighting".
