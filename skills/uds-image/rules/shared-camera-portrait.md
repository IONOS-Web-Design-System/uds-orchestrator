# Camera presets — portrait

## waist-up-eye-level-three-quarter
waist-up at eye level, subject turned three-quarter to the lens, normal lens, medium-depth background

## waist-up-eye-level-frontal
waist-up at eye level, squared frontally to the lens, normal lens, readable background at medium depth

## tight-chest-up-longlens
tight chest-up at eye level, three-quarter turn, long lens with visible compression, strong background blur and bokeh highlights

## low-angle-hero
waist-up from slightly below eye level looking up, wide-normal lens, the subject reading tall against the space above

## high-angle-relaxed
chest-up from slightly above eye level looking down, subject glancing back up toward the lens, normal lens, informal framing

## over-shoulder-turned
from behind and slightly off-axis, the subject turning back over the shoulder so the face is partly in frame, normal lens

## full-body-environmental
full body at eye level, wide lens, the subject occupying a modest part of a legible environment, deep focus throughout

## profile-side-on
strict side-on profile at eye level, medium lens, the subject looking out of frame along their eyeline

## knee-up-standing-wide
standing knee-up three-quarter, wide-normal lens, a foreground element entering one frame edge for depth

## close-shallow-offgaze
close waist-up at eye level, very shallow depth of field, gaze directed off-camera past the lens
